# Brand Identity: Lina's Portfolio

## 🌿 The Essence: "Fluid Craft"
The brand identity is rooted in the raw, organic beauty of **recycled paper** and the fluid transparency of **watercolor art**. It balances the tactile, fibrous nature of physical materials with the ethereal, bleeding boundaries of liquid pigment. It's grounded in sustainability and artistic expression.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette
The colors are derived from botanical dyes and diluted pigments.

### Foundational (The Recycled Canvas)
- **Paper (`#F9F7F2`)**: The primary background. A warm, cream-toned recycled paper base.
- **Paper Base (`#EBE7DE`)**: A denser, more fibrous tone used for layered depth.
- **Ink (`#121212`)**: A soft, charcoal-like black that mimics carbon-based ink.

### Emotional (Organic Pigments)
- **Moss Dark (`#2C3B24`)**: Deep botanical green, representing growth and grounding.
- **Terra Orange (`#D2691E`)**: Muted clay/terracotta, adding warmth and creative energy.

### Accents (The Watercolor Wash)
- **Watercolor Blue (`#B3D9E6`)**: A transparent wash of sky and water.
- **Watercolor Rose (`#FADADD`)**: A delicate, diluted petal pink.
- **Watercolor Ochre (`#E3B448`)**: Sun-drenched earth, used for highlights.

---

## 🖋️ Typography
A contrast between the structured and the organic.

- **Serif: Playfair Display**: Used for high-impact headers. It evokes the history of printing and traditional craft.
- **Handwriting: Caveat**: Used for annotations and personal signatures, mimicking the artist's hand.
- **Sans: Inter**: Used for functional UI, providing modern clarity against the organic background.

---

## 📐 Design Principles

### 1. Organic Texture (Recycled Paper)
- **Fibrous Grain**: We use subtle noise and SVG turbulence to mimic the uneven surface of recycled fibers.
- **Deckled Edges**: Cards and sections feature torn, deckled edges instead of clean digital cuts.
- **Layered Depth**: Elements overlap with soft shadows, suggesting physical layers of paper.

### 2. Pigment Fluidity (Watercolor)
- **Ink Bleed & Wash**: Visual boundaries are never hard; we use SVG filters and blurs to simulate pigment spreading into wet paper.
- **Transparency**: Watercolor accents are layered with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` to let the paper texture show through.
- **Organic Shapes**: Using non-geometric, fluid paths for backgrounds and highlights.

### 3. Balanced Asymmetry
- **Natural Composition**: Layouts follow organic paths rather than rigid grids, allowing content to "float" naturally on the page.
- **Staggered Elements**: Intentionally breaking alignment to reinforce the hand-crafted feel.

---

## ✨ Motion & Interaction
- **Liquid Transitions**: Page changes should feel like a fresh wash of color settling onto a page.
- **Tactile Feedback**: Hover states emphasize the texture, making elements feel like they are being lifted or pressed.
- **Spring Physics**: Movement is organic and responsive, avoiding linear or mechanical paths.
